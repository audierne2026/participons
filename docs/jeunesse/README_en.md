# RAG Context File – Audierne 2026

## Category: School & Youth

Version 1.0 – February 05, 2026  
Main source: Audierne 2026 participatory program section

### Key Observations

- Importance of quality educational support.
- Mobility challenges for young people.
- Need for diversified extracurricular activities.

### Current Proposals (participatory – evolving)

1. **Support for public schools**
   - Well-adapted and maintained facilities.
   - Ongoing dialogue with teaching teams and parents.

2. **Activities for youth**
   - Development of extracurricular and leisure offers.
   - Dedicated spaces for teenagers.

### Complementary Local Context

- Main schools: Public Pierre Le Lec (nursery + primary + ULIS, major renovation planned 2026-2027 with courtyard greening), Public Esquibien school.
- Private schools: Sainte-Anne (Catholic), Diwan (Breton language), others in Cap Sizun.
- College: Bois de Locqueran (Audierne), Lamennais (private).
- Leisure centre (ALSH): Managed by Cap Sizun Animation (ages 3-12, Wednesdays + holidays, Audierne sites and nearby).
- Ongoing projects: Pierre Le Lec school renovation (start early 2026, ready for 2027), past debates on school groupings, rural schools network Grand Cap.
- Youth mobility: rural school transport issues, SIJ (Youth Information Structure) permanent office opened in Audierne (2025).
- Youth activities: surfing, sports, culture via Cap Sizun associations.
