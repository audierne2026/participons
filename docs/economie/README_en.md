# RAG Context File – Audierne 2026

## Category: Local Economy

Version 1.0 – February 05, 2026  
Main source: Audierne 2026 participatory program section

### Key Observations

- Fragile local proximity commerce
- Tourism to be enhanced while preserving quality of life
- Need for support to local entrepreneurs

### Current Proposals (participatory – evolving)

1. **Boost local commerce**
   - Support for installation of proximity shops
   - Animation of the town center
   - Short supply chains and local consumption

2. **Sustainable tourism**
   - Promotion of respectful tourism
   - Valorization of maritime and cultural heritage
   - Balance between tourist activity and local life

3. **Local artisanal fishing**
   - Support to the artisanal fishing industry
   - Valorization of local products
   - Creation of a network of actors linking fishing, restaurants and food sectors

### Complementary Local Context

- Cap Sizun economic portrait: ~2600 businesses (incl. social economy), ~3849 jobs (73.7% salaried), key sectors: population services, agriculture/fishing, commerce, tourism; activity zones (Audierne-Kerguerrien, Pont-Croix-Lanéon).
- Commerce & crafts: active merchants association (town center dynamization, Commerce & Crafts Pass for takeovers/installations, attractive weekly markets Wed/Sat).
- Economic tourism: Weeks of Economic Tourism (business visits, artisans, brewers, local know-how), professional fishing port essential.
- Fishing & local products: Audierne port (landings, auction), Capucins Beach seasonal market (local producers), PAT Cap Sizun (local food, short circuits).
- Other: Petites Villes de Demain program (quality of life/economy improvement), Cap Sizun Entrepreneurs Collective (network), seasonal/tourism jobs.
