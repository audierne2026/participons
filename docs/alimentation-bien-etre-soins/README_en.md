# RAG Context File – Audierne 2026

## Category: Food, Well-being & Care

Version 1.0 – February 05, 2026  
Main source: Audierne 2026 participatory program section

### Key Observations

- Strong initiatives for local food: “Manger demain” fortnight (April 2025) with workshops, farm visits, solidarity gleaning; participation in Alimenterre festival with screenings-debates (November 2025); annual short-circuit producers catalogue updated.
- Growing organic and healthy products offer: stores like Biocoop Maison Biologique in Audierne, focusing on local and fair-trade products.
- Increasing needs in well-being and care: aging population with high demand for home help (ADMR supports over 260 seniors); well-being initiatives (Reiki, relaxation, coastal walks).
- Potential for more social links: intergenerational workshops, shared gardens, health prevention to enhance quality of life.

### Current Proposals (participatory – evolving)

1. **Local and sustainable food**
   - Strengthen short circuits and producers catalogue
   - Support events like food fortnight and Alimenterre
   - Develop regular organic markets and solidarity grocery stores

2. **Daily well-being**
   - Promote accessible physical activities (coastal walks, yoga, relaxation workshops)
   - Create green spaces and shared gardens for social links
   - Valorize local offers (Reiki, naturopathy, health sports)

3. **Access to care and support**
   - Strengthen home help for seniors (ADMR, coordinated services)
   - Improve access to health professionals and prevention
   - Develop intergenerational initiatives to fight isolation

### Complementary Local Context

- Territorial Food Project (PAT) Cap Sizun – Pointe du Raz: promotion of healthy, sustainable, local food; launched with Itinerant Food House (2024); citizen meetings (2025); short-circuit producers catalogue (2025 edition).
- Food events: “Manger demain” fortnight (April 2025: farm visits, workshops, organic tastings); Alimenterre festival (screenings-debates).
- Organic/local supply: Biocoop Maison Biologique (Audierne, local/fair products); solidarity grocery stores; Restos du Cœur Audierne (food aid).
- Well-being & care: ADMR Audierne Sud Cap Sizun (home help >260 seniors); Multi-professional Health Center West Cap Sizun (health professionals grouping); yoga/health activities (Cap Yoga Santé), Chinese energy care, reflexology, prevention (personalized welcome space).
- Social links & prevention: shared gardens, intergenerational workshops, aging well (CLIC guide), Cap Sizun health permanencies.
