# RAG Context File – Audierne 2026

## Category: Environment

Version 1.0 – February 05, 2026  
Main source: Audierne 2026 participatory program section

### Key Observations

- Exceptional natural heritage to preserve
- Climate emergency requiring local actions
- Waste management to be optimized

### Current Proposals (participatory – evolving)

1. **Local ecological transition**
   - Reduction of municipal carbon footprint
   - Support for soft mobility
   - Protection of natural areas

2. **Resource management**
   - Optimization of waste collection and sorting
   - Promotion of composting and circular economy
   - Preservation of water resources

### Complementary Local Context

- Natural heritage: exceptional coastline (baie d'Audierne, Pointe du Raz Grand Site de France, Natura 2000 Cap Sizun, wetlands, ecological continuities).
- Ecological transition: ongoing **PCAET** (Territorial Climate-Air-Energy Plan) by CC Cap Sizun – Pointe du Raz (2024 diagnostic, actions towards 2050: climate adaptation, biodiversity, energy, flood reduction).
- Biodiversity & protection: management of sensitive natural areas (Conservatoire du littoral, Bretagne Vivante), coastal waste collection points, DOCOB Cap Sizun, potential Ramsar label for baie d'Audierne.
- Waste: improved sorting/collection, composting, circular economy; reminders on yellow bags, costly green waste.
- Other issues: carbon footprint reduction, soft mobility, water preservation, limited soil artificialization (PLU, environmental assessments).
