# RAG Context File – Audierne 2026

## Category: Culture & Heritage

Version 1.0 – February 05, 2026  
Main source: Audierne 2026 participatory program section

### Key Observations

- Exceptional richness of religious heritage: several classified chapels (Sainte-Brigitte dated 1654, Sainte-Edwett with only classified furniture - 1723) and historic churches (Saint-Raymond, Saint-Onneau, Saint-Joseph)
- Safeguarding and modernization of the Cap-Sizun Maritime Museum
- Unique prehistoric and maritime heritage: menhirs, dolmens at Sainte-Evette, historic port at the heart of Audierne identity
- Dynamic cultural associative life: Audierne Esquibien Culture association regularly organizes exhibitions, conferences and animations (Science Festival, Le Goyen cinema)
- Appreciated but improvable cultural events: Music Festival, “Goyen en lumières” illuminations, summer festivals attract residents and visitors
- Need to enhance and transmit this heritage to younger generations and newcomers

### Current Proposals (participatory – evolving)

1. **Strengthen heritage enhancement**
   - Creation of guided heritage circuits (chapels, fountains, historic port) with modern signage and QR codes
   - Strengthened partnership with Audierne Esquibien Culture for an annual program of exhibitions and conferences
   - Nighttime lighting of emblematic sites (Saint-Raymond church, chapels)

2. **Develop cultural offer for all audiences**
   - Increased support for local cultural associations (associative libraries, theater, music)
   - Regular programming of concerts, youth shows and festivals (extension of Music Festival, creation of a maritime festival)
   - Intergenerational workshops around Breton heritage (storytelling, dance, language)

3. **Safeguard and modernize the Cap-Sizun Maritime Museum**
   - Active support to the association to find suitable and sustainable premises (renovation or new sea-facing site)
   - Public-private funding to digitize collections and create interactive exhibitions
   - Integration of the museum into a maritime heritage circuit (link with port, lighthouses, chapels)

### Complementary Local Context

- Religious & built heritage: Saint-Joseph church (MH), Saint-Raymond, chapels Sainte-Brigitte (1654), Sainte-Edwett; historic port, towpath, sailors' shelter, Fénoux mast, Capucins footbridge.
- Cap-Sizun Maritime Museum: housed in former hospice, collections on local maritime life; need for modernization and new premises.
- Audierne Esquibien Culture association: exhibitions (e.g. Cap Sizun coal mines, historic station), conferences, animations (Science Festival), old station model in town hall.
- Events & cultural tourism: guided heritage tours (tourism office), Music Festival, Goyen en lumières illuminations, Weeks of Economic Tourism, Aquashow (nearby aquarium), Tréouzien Mill.
- Prehistoric/maritime heritage: menhirs/dolmens Sainte-Evette, port of patrimonial interest, Classified Tourist Resort label.
- Other: General Inventory of Cultural Heritage (Bretagne), interpretation circuits, Cap Sizun cultural heritage (exceptional old buildings).
